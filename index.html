Three things

How it works

01 · GRIND

Keys, until one fits

A Solana address is just a public key. Your browser generates them on every core and checks the last letters, millions of times, until one ends the way you asked.

02 · SEAL

That key becomes your mint

The winning key signs the create transaction as the coin's mint, so the coin's CA is that address. You sign the same transaction as creator.

03 · LIVE

A normal StonkFun coin

It's created on Raydium LaunchLab under the StonkFun platform with STONK as the pair, so it lives on stonkfun.xyz like any other launch.

Safety: the mint key is generated in your browser tab and used only to sign your launch, then dropped. It is never uploaded. SIGNET's server only pins your image and builds the unsigned transaction. You are the creator and you get the creator fees, exactly as if you launched on StonkFun directly.
